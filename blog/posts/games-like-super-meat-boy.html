7 Steam Games Like Super Meat Boy for Fans of Short Levels and Fast Retries

If you like Super Meat Boy for its short courses and fast retries, start with iZBOT for double jumps and wall slides, RITE for bite-sized key-and-exit trials, or N++ for momentum-based jumps. For quick retries inside a longer adventure, try Celeste or The End Is Nigh.

Ruxar developed and publishes iZBOT. The picks below are organised by the kind of movement and practice each game rewards.

What happens after you miss a jump?

There are two parts to a fast retry: how soon you get control back and how much ground you have to cover before you reach the jump again. This table compares where a typical retry puts you and what you replay in the main modes. Challenge modes can have different rules.

1. iZBOT — Short courses with wall slides and double jumps

A missed jump sends you back to the start of the current course. The immediate retry lets you adjust a single part of the route, such as sliding a little farther down a wall before jumping or saving your second jump to clear the next hazard. Pick iZBOT if you like practising an entire short course until the moves fit together.

View iZBOT on Steam

2. RITE — Fast key-and-exit trials

Each trial gives you a key to reach and an exit to find. The optional gold gives you a reason to stray from the safest path. Pick RITE if you want compact pixel-art stages where a clear route can become a more ambitious one on the next run.

RITE on Steam

3. N++ — Momentum and route optimisation

Your approach to an obstacle sets up the next jump. Build speed, judge the arc and decide whether grabbing extra gold justifies a detour. Standard episodes group levels together, and Hardcore mode raises the stakes with episode-level restarts.

N++ on Steam

4. Celeste — Room-by-room challenges with a story

Celeste gives you room to plan where to spend your air dash, where to land and where to recover it. You can practise a difficult room without replaying the climb that brought you there. Pick it if you want short bursts of precision inside a longer, story-driven adventure.

Celeste on Steam

5. The End Is Nigh — Short screens in a connected world

Ash can grab ledges to negotiate cramped routes. Each screen gives you another small platforming problem, and collectible tumours can tempt you into tougher routes. Pick it if you want quick retries alongside exploration.

The End Is Nigh on Steam

6. VVVVVV — Precision without a jump button

Flip gravity from a surface and steer as you fall. The challenge is choosing when to reverse direction and how far to drift before you hit the next surface. Pick it if you like quick feedback and want a different movement skill to master.

VVVVVV on Steam

7. Electronic Super Joy — Checkpointed challenges with electronic music

Low gravity, rotating levels and missiles change how you approach each stretch. Checkpoints give you smaller sections to practise within longer courses. Pick it if you like fast obstacle changes and a loud electronic soundtrack.

Electronic Super Joy on Steam

Which game is closest to Super Meat Boy?

Start with iZBOT if the appeal is learning short, self-contained courses through repeated attempts. Choose N++ if you spend more time chasing a smoother route, or Celeste if you want difficult jumps broken into rooms along a longer climb.
