Pixel-Art Robot Platformers on Steam: Which One Should You Play?

Play iZBOT for fast, arcade-style platforming with double jumps and wall slides. Pick Super Magbot if you want to solve movement puzzles with magnets, Haiku, the Robot for a world to explore, or Mega Man Legacy Collection for classic shooting and boss fights.

Ruxar developed and publishes iZBOT. All six picks put you in control of a robot and use pixel art. The comparison focuses on what you’ll spend your time doing and how each game is structured.

iZBOT — Fast arcade platforming

Pick iZBOT if you want to get straight to a difficult jump and try it again as soon as you miss. A wall slide gives you time to line up the next move, and saving the second jump lets you adjust where you land. The short stages make it easy to spend a session learning a single route or pushing through several.

View iZBOT on Steam

BZZZT — Dash-driven precision

BZZZT puts a tiny robot named ZX8000 through a series of platforming challenges. The dash is the draw: you work out where to commit to a burst of speed and where to slow down for the landing. Pick it if you like compact obstacle courses and colourful arcade-style pixel art.

BZZZT on Steam

Super Magbot — Movement puzzles

Magbot is too heavy to jump, so you pull toward surfaces or launch away from them using magnetic polarity. The route asks two questions before every gap: which magnetic surface gets you across, and where will its force send you? Pick this if figuring out how to move sounds as satisfying as executing the route.

Super Magbot on Steam

Haiku, the Robot — Exploration and melee combat

Haiku explores Arcadia, a world of damaged machines drawn in Game Boy-inspired pixel art. New abilities open ways into previously inaccessible areas, while melee combat and bosses punctuate the exploration. Pick it if you want to come back to a locked route hours later with the ability that makes it click.

Haiku, the Robot on Steam

Robot Wants It All — Small Metroidvania maps

The Robot Wants games give each adventure a simple goal, such as reaching a kitty, then make you collect the powers to get there. Each adventure has its own map and progression. Pick this if you like the satisfaction of opening shortcuts and revisiting obstacles in smaller doses.

Robot Wants It All on Steam

Mega Man Legacy Collection — Classic run-and-gun stages

Capcom’s collection brings the original six Mega Man games to Steam. Stages ask you to dodge enemies, place jumps and learn boss patterns. Pick it if you want classic robot action where which weapon you bring matters alongside how well you move.

Mega Man Legacy Collection on Steam

Which robot platformers work well for short sessions?

Start with iZBOT or BZZZT if you want a few compact stages to practise in one sitting. Robot Wants It All is the pick if you’d rather spend that session exploring a small map and unlocking a route. For more games grouped by how much time they take to finish, see our guide to short platformers on Steam.

What should you wishlist for a Steam sale?

If you want short, hazard-filled courses and instant retries, check iZBOT’s price or add it to your Steam wishlist. Choose Super Magbot if you want a different way to cross each gap, or Haiku if you want your next game to give you a world to come back to.
