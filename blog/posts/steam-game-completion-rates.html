Steam Game Completion Rates: Six Achievement Milestones

How many Steam players reach a game's ending? There is no single platform-wide completion rate. For six selected games, Steam's public global achievement pages show milestone unlock rates ranging from 17.6% to 46.7% in the figures checked for this article.

Each figure below is the percentage Steam displays for one named achievement. The games are an illustrative selection across several genres, not a random sample of Steam games. The milestones differ, so the table is useful for examining definitions as well as numbers.

What do these percentages mean?

Steam labels these figures as the percentage of players who unlocked an achievement. They are achievement unlock rates, not a count of purchasers, an estimate of hours played, or a survey of why people stopped playing. Steam does not publish the underlying player count beside each percentage on these pages.

“Completion” changes meaning by game. Hades tracks a first successful run. Terraria has no single campaign ending in the same sense as Portal 2. Hollow Knight has multiple ending routes, and the row above measures only the named route. Cuphead's selected achievement specifies Normal difficulty. A higher rate here does not prove a game is easier or better.

Method and limitations

We selected six well-known Steam games with a public achievement that marks a clear major milestone. For each one, we recorded Steam's displayed global percentage and the achievement name, then classified what that milestone represents. We excluded games with no suitably clear milestone. The linked source pages and the source table (CSV) make the comparison reproducible.

The selection is illustrative and cannot establish an average completion rate for Steam, a genre, or a release year. The six percentages have different player populations and milestone definitions; we therefore do not average them. Achievement systems can also change after release, and unlocking an achievement is not guaranteed to capture every playthrough. Recheck Steam before quoting a current rate.

How to use the data when choosing a game

Start with the milestone description, then read the percentage. A first escape, a story ending, and a final boss answer different questions. If you're choosing a game for a satisfying challenge, also consider its retry structure and your preferred session length. Our precision platformer guide compares that side of the experience.
