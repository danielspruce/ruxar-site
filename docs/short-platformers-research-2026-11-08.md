# Research brief: highly rated short platformers

Researched 8 November 2026. Research only; no article or site changes made.

## Proposed title and purpose

**6 Highly Rated Short Platformers for a Weekend**

Primary topic: best short platformers. Related query candidates: short platformers on Steam, short platform games, platformers to finish in a weekend. These are editorial query targets, not verified volume or difficulty figures.

Audience: players choosing a compact game they can actually finish, rather than developers or players looking only for short individual levels. The information gain over a generic list is separating campaign length, optional completion, platformer subtype, and the effect of difficulty on the time commitment.

Use highly rated rather than top ranked: this is a curated selection, not an exhaustive Steam leaderboard. Steam's extracted scores use English reviews for five games and all reviews for Toree 2; do not sort these into a purported universal numeric ranking. Scores are dated snapshots returned by Steam retrieval, not guaranteed real-time counts.

## Recommended article structure

1. Direct opening: six compact platformers, with campaign estimates distinguished from 100% completion.
2. Explain selection: positive Steam reception, evidence of short campaigns, variety of platforming styles. Curated, not an exhaustive ranking.
3. Comparison table: game, approximate reported duration, movement/style, what extends playtime.
4. Group by time commitment: about an hour; a couple of hours; a longer evening or weekend.
5. One concise entry each: actual mechanic, who might enjoy it, time caveat and source. No claim of personal play unless verified.
6. Answer whether short means easy and whether 100% completion fits the same time budget.

Keep this distinct from the existing Super Meat Boy alternatives article: that article is about fast retries; this one is about the total commitment needed to reach an ending.

Do not insert iZBOT into a claimed top-rated ranking to promote it. If mentioned, use a small clearly disclosed related-game note and avoid unsupported completion-time promises. Do not pad the list with Celeste, N++, Super Meat Boy or Hollow Knight simply because some individual levels or attempts are short.

For a published article, keep all Steam destination UTMs unique per page/placement under the site's existing convention. No store pricing needed for this angle; a price claim would need another dated check.

### Interpretation and article decisions

- Replace the initial INSIDE estimate of around five hours with approximately 3.5 hours for Main Story, attributed to this supplied HowLongToBeat snapshot. Five hours remains a particular reviewer's experience.
- Gato Roboto now has a screenshot-supported community Main Story average of 3h 27m, rather than relying only on a few divergent reviews.
- Toree 2's approximately one-hour reviewer estimate included additional objectives. Its screenshot Main Story average is 33 minutes; Main + Sides average is 1h 10m. Keep those categories distinct.
- VVVVVV is short for the main route, but its Completionist average exceeds five hours. Avoid claiming that every game can be completed 100% within five hours.
- iZBOT and iZBOT 2 now have documented duration evidence, but only four and one Main Story submissions respectively. They can be discussed as short-game candidates with those sample sizes disclosed; they do not justify a confident typical-player estimate or a claim that the sequel takes longer.
- Completionist averages for the two iZBOT games are shorter than Main Story averages because they describe different submission groups. Do not present this as proof that full completion is faster than finishing the story, and do not pool categories into a synthetic duration.
- The ratings displayed on HowLongToBeat (for example iZBOT's 62% and iZBOT 2's 50%) are that site's ratings, not Steam positive-review percentages. Keep reception sourced separately from Steam.
- Retain the six independent recommendations as the core highly-rated shortlist, with a short disclosed Ruxar section for iZBOT and iZBOT 2 if desired. A broader article including all eight could use the less ranking-oriented title: Short Platformers on Steam: Main Story and Completion Times.
- LIMBO has no screenshot in this batch; retain its reviewer-based estimate, clearly distinguish its source, or leave it out of a table described as entirely sourced from the supplied screenshots.
